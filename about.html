Our Story

RogueByte Studios began in a cramped apartment with three friends, one laptop, and a shared belief that indie games could rival blockbuster experiences in heart and ambition.

What started as late-night game jams and weekend prototypes evolved into a full-time studio. Today, we are a distributed team across four continents, working on multiple titles that span cyberpunk cities, alien frontiers, and shadowy espionage worlds.

We believe great games come from bold ideas, relentless iteration, and a deep respect for the player. Every project we take on is a chance to push ourselves — and the medium — forward.

Our Mission & Values

The principles that guide every world we build.

Purposeful Design

Every system, scene, and sound exists for a reason. We cut the fluff and chase the feeling.

Creative Courage

We take risks on weird ideas. The best moments in games come from unexpected places.

Player Respect

No dark patterns, no pay-to-win. We build trust by putting the player experience first.

Quality Obsession

We polish until it hurts. A game is only ready when every moment feels intentional.

Sustainable Pace

Great games are made by healthy teams. We avoid crunch and invest in our people.

Continuous Growth

Every project teaches us something new. We share knowledge and level up together.
